Mix, effects & monitoring

Left is −100, centre is 0, right is +100. Recordings preserve these stereo positions.

Drive, delay and reverb affect the whole mix. A master limiter sits at the end to keep enthusiastic knob-twiddling from becoming a public safety issue.

Live master-output oscilloscope. Change waveform, filter, attack or release and you can see the result as well as hear it.

Recording, mixing & arrangement

Positive values skip that many milliseconds from the start of the loaded track. Negative values delay it. Battleship auto-detects leading recorder silence when a track is loaded, then you can nudge it in 1 ms steps.

Ready to record

Manual record is still available. Exact Record + Play gives a 4-beat count-in, then records the master output as PCM against the Web Audio clock and produces a WAV whose sample 0 is the musical downbeat. This avoids MediaRecorder/WebM/Opus preroll entirely. Battleship-made WAVs therefore reload at 0 ms trim; the trim control remains for older WebM recordings. A short safety tail prevents hard-cut clicks. Clear current removes the new take from this panel; Unload track removes the loaded overdub source. Saved files on your phone are untouched.
