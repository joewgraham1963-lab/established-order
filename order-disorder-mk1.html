Your move. Communication now travels in straight lines from arsenals and relays.
How this version works. Each side has two arsenals. Arsenals radiate communication horizontally, vertically and diagonally until mountains or enemy pieces block the line. Relays that are themselves connected radiate further lines. A unit can act if it is on a communication line or adjacent, including diagonally, to a connected friendly unit. Infantry, artillery and relays move 1 square; cavalry moves up to 2. Moving onto an enemy captures it. Capture both enemy arsenals to win.
This is still a simplified browser adaptation, not a reproduction of Debord's full combat system.
