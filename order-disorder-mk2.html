Choose PLAY BRAIN, HOST ONLINE, or JOIN.
Three rules:
1. Move one connected unit one square in any direction, including diagonally.
2. Communication travels up, down, left and right only. A unit cut off from its base goes grey and cannot move.
3. Capture the enemy base, disconnect all enemy units, or leave the enemy with no legal move to win.
Online mode is two-player head-to-head. One person hosts and sends the code to the other. It uses a direct browser connection, so both players need internet access.
