Internet Colours

Websites often describe colours using a hexadecimal colour code. It begins with a hash symbol and contains six characters.

The basic pattern is:

#RRGGBB

  • RR controls red.
  • GG controls green.
  • BB controls blue.

The values run from 00, meaning none of that colour, to ff, meaning the maximum amount.

#ffffff — white
#000000 — black
#848484 — grey
#ff8800 — orange

Try your own colour

#848484
Red: 132
Green: 132
Blue: 132

Why is #848484 grey?

Its red, green and blue values are all equal. Equal amounts of red, green and blue produce a shade of grey.

Lower matching values make darker greys. Higher matching values make lighter greys.

#222222 is dark grey, #848484 is a middle grey, and #e8e8e8 is light grey.
